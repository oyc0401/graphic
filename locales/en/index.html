Your image never leaves your device

PaintOn has no upload step. Files you open are decoded and edited locally in your browser with WebGL2, so photos of documents, faces, or anything private stay exactly where they are — on your machine.

Frequently asked questions

Is PaintOn free?

Yes. PaintOn is completely free. There is no account, no sign-up, and nothing to install — it opens directly in your browser.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All editing runs locally in your browser using WebGL2. Images you open or paste are processed on your device and are never uploaded.

What editing tools does PaintOn have?

Brush, pencil, and eraser; shapes and color fill; rectangular and freeform selection with move and transform; liquify with push, rotate, bloat, pucker, and restore; pixel and blur mosaic; undo/redo history; and PNG export including transparent backgrounds.

Does PaintOn work on phones and tablets?

Yes. PaintOn has a touch-friendly mobile UI with pinch zoom and multi-touch gestures, and it also works on desktop browsers.
